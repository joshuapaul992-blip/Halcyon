Screen savers

A screen saver is a moving picture that takes over the screen when you have not touched the mouse or keyboard for a while. It hides what is on your screen from passers-by and is nice to look at. Move the mouse or press any key and you are right back where you were.

Choosing a screen saver

  1. Open Display Properties and click the Screen Saver tab.
  2. Pick a screen saver from the drop-down list. The small monitor in the dialog shows it running.
  3. Set Wait to the number of minutes without activity before it starts (type it, or use the little arrows).
  4. Click Preview to see it full screen (move the mouse or press a key to come back), then OK.

The key, click or mouse movement that ends a screen saver only ends it: it does not reach the program underneath, so nothing is typed or clicked by accident. A small wobble of the mouse does not count. While a program shows something full screen, or Media Player plays, the screen saver waits.

Halcyon comes with these screen savers:

BlankA plain black screen.
StarfieldFly through a field of stars.
Drifting LinesColourful lines that wander across the screen.
Halcyon LogoThe Halcyon logo bouncing gently from edge to edge.
Scrolling TextYour own message gliding across the screen.

Choose (None) if you never want a screen saver.

Your own message

With Scrolling Text selected, click Settings to type the message and choose its font, size (1x to 4x), colour (plain, gradients or a rainbow) and speed; the strip at the bottom of that window shows the result as you go. The other screen savers have no options.

For the curious

Screen savers are small programs with the extension .SCR in C:\PROGRAMS; double-click one in Explorer to see it full screen. Your choice and the wait time are kept in SETTINGS.INI (screensaver.file, screensaver.wait), like all Halcyon settings.
