Getting started with Halcyon

Halcyon is a small operating system with a friendly, early-2000s look: a desktop with icons, a Start menu, windows you can move around, and a handful of useful programs. If you have used Windows XP, you will feel at home straight away. This page walks you through the basics.

Your first look around

  • The big picture behind everything is the desktop. Its icons open My Computer, My Documents, Notepad, Paint and other programs. See The desktop and the Start menu.
  • The blue bar along the bottom is the taskbar. It has the Start button on the left, a button for each open window, and the clock on the right.
  • Click Start to see everything you can do: open programs, your documents, the Control Panel, this Help, and turn the computer off.

Using the mouse

ClickPress and release the left button once: selects an icon or presses a button.
Double-clickTwo quick clicks: opens a file, folder or program.
Right-clickPress the right button: shows a menu of things you can do with what is under the pointer.
DragHold the left button down while you move the mouse: moves windows, selects text, moves files.
WheelScrolls the window under the pointer up and down.

Touchpads and tablets work too. Most things can also be done with the keyboard: see Keyboard shortcuts.

Opening and saving your work

Your own files live in My Documents (the folder C:\Documents). Programs such as Notepad and Paint start their Open and Save dialogs there. To look through your files, open My Computer or My Documents: see Files and folders in Explorer.

Turning the computer off

Click Start, then Turn Off, and choose Turn Off or Restart. Halcyon writes everything to the disks, saves its boot log and then switches the computer off. Please do not just pull the plug or the USB stick while Halcyon is running: files you were working on might not have been written yet.

Which version am I running?

Click Start and look below the list of programs, or choose About Halcyon. The version is 1.0 followed by a build number. Until version 1.0 is finished, every build is a preview, and the desktop says "For testing purposes only" in its bottom right corner.
