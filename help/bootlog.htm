The boot log

While Halcyon runs, it writes down what it is doing: which devices it found, which drivers started, which drives it mounted, and any problems along the way. This record is the boot log. It is the most useful thing to send along when you report a problem.

Where to find it

  • C:\HALCYON\BOOT.LOG holds the log of the current (or last) session. Halcyon saves it when the desktop appears, when you turn the computer off or restart, and after a crash.
  • C:\HALCYON\BOOTPREV.LOG is the log of the session before. Handy when the last start went wrong and you started again.

Both are plain text: open them with Notepad. Because they are on the USB stick, you can also read them on another computer.

Looking at the log while Halcyon runs

  • In System Information, click Kernel Log....
  • In the Command Prompt, DMESG shows the latest messages (DMESG 50: the last 50), and BOOTLOG saves the log to the stick right now.
  • To watch the messages during start-up, choose Verbose boot in the F8 menu.

Reporting a problem

Say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, and attach BOOT.LOG (and BOOTPREV.LOG if the problem was in the session before). The log contains no personal files, only what Halcyon found on the computer and what it did.
