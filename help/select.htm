Selecting several files

Many jobs are quicker when you do them to several files at once: copy a whole set of photos, delete a batch of old notes, or move a few documents to a USB stick. Explorer lets you select as many files and folders as you like, and then copy, move, drag or delete them together.

With the mouse

Ctrl+clickAdds an item to the selection, or takes it out again.
Shift+clickSelects everything from the last item you clicked to this one.
Drag a rectangleStart on an empty part of the folder and drag: everything inside the rectangle is selected. Hold Ctrl to add to what is already selected.
Click an empty spotClears the selection.

With the keyboard

Ctrl+ASelects everything in the folder.
Shift+arrow keysExtends the selection from the current item.
Ctrl+arrow keysMoves the focus without changing the selection; Ctrl+Space then selects or deselects the item with the focus.

Working with the selection

Once you have selected some items, Copy, Cut, Delete and dragging work on all of them together. The status bar shows how many items are selected and their total size.
