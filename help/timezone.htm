Date, time and time zone

The clock in the taskbar shows the time from your computer's own clock. The Control Panel shows the date and time too, in its Date and Time box.

Setting the time zone

Computers often keep their clock in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), the time in London in winter. Halcyon needs to know where you are to show the right time, and it also uses the time zone to check the dates of secure web sites.

  1. Open the Control Panel.
  2. In the Date and Time box, click the time zone button.
  3. Choose the difference between your computer's clock and UTC. Each entry names some places that use it, such as Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Vladivostok.
  4. Click OK.

Summer time

Halcyon does not change the clock for summer time (daylight saving time) by itself yet. Where the clocks change for summer, choose the offset that is in effect now, and change it again when the clocks go back.

The time is still wrong

If Windows is also installed on the computer, it may have set the computer's clock to local time rather than UTC. Choose the time zone that makes Halcyon's clock right; Windows will not be affected.
