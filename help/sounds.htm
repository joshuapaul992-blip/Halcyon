Sounds and Audio Devices

This window controls everything you hear. Open it from the Control Panel (Sounds...), or double-click the speaker icon next to the clock.

Volume

Drag the slider to set the volume for the whole computer, or tick Mute to silence it. These change straight away (Cancel puts them back). For a quick change, click the speaker icon in the notification area, or use the volume keys on your keyboard. Untick Place volume icon in the taskbar if you would rather not have the icon.

Sound devices

The list shows the sound cards Halcyon found, with their driver and what they are doing. Halcyon works with the sound built into most PCs since about 2005 (High Definition Audio) as well as older AC'97 sound and a few classic cards. With headphones plugged in, the sound moves to them and the speakers go quiet, just as you would expect.

System sounds

Halcyon plays short sounds of its own when it starts and shuts down, when a message box appears, when a balloon pops up, and when you plug in or remove a USB device. In the Sounds box you can:

  • Choose a sound scheme: Halcyon is the standard set, Halcyon Dusk is a calmer set with a longer startup tune and a gentler goodbye, and Halcyon Tide is a bright set of quick mallet notes. A scheme you choose is used at once, so Play lets you hear it before you decide; OK or Apply keeps it and Cancel goes back to the scheme you had.
  • Untick Play system sounds for silence.
  • Select an event, then click Play to hear it, untick Enabled to silence just that one, Browse... to use another WAV file for it, or Default to go back to Halcyon's own sound.

No sound?

  1. Check that the speaker icon is not crossed out (muted) and the volume is up.
  2. Check that the speakers are switched on and plugged into the right socket.
  3. Look in Sound devices. If no sound card is listed, Halcyon has no driver for yours yet: it will use the PC speaker for beeps. Please report it, with your boot log.
