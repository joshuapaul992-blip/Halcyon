Mouse Properties

Mouse Properties sets how your mouse behaves. Open the Control Panel and click Mouse.... Changes work at once so you can try them; click OK or Apply to keep them (also after a restart), or Cancel to go back to how it was.

Button configuration

Tick Switch primary and secondary buttons if you use the mouse with your left hand: the right button then selects and drags, and the left button opens menus. The picture of the mouse shows which button is the primary one.

Double-click speed

Move the slider towards Slow if your double-clicks are often taken as two single clicks, or towards Fast if two separate clicks open things. Double-click the folder next to the slider to try it: it opens or closes when the double-click counts.

Pointer speed

The slider sets how far the pointer moves for a given movement of the mouse. (A tablet or a touch screen points straight at the spot and is not affected.)
