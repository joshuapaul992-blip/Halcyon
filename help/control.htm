Control Panel

The Control Panel is where you make Halcyon your own. Open it from the Start menu, or open it now.

Theme and wallpaper

The Control Panel window itself lets you change how Halcyon looks:

  • Theme sets the colours of the title bars, the taskbar and the Start menu: Halcyon Blue, Evergreen or Graphite.
  • Wallpaper picks the picture behind your desktop icons: Halcyon Bay, Aurora, Dunes or plain Solid Teal. A small preview shows your choice.

Click Apply to try a change while the Control Panel stays open, OK to apply it and close, or Cancel to leave things as they were. The panel also shows the date and time and some facts about your computer.

More settings

The buttons along the bottom open the other settings windows:

Display...Screen resolution and the screen saver.
Keyboard...Keyboard layouts for other languages.
Mouse...Swapping the mouse buttons, double-click and pointer speed.
Network...Your network connection.
Sounds...Volume, sound cards and system sounds.
The time zone buttonIn the Date and Time box: where in the world you are, for the clock.

Where are my settings kept?

Halcyon keeps every choice you make in a small text file, C:\HALCYON\SETTINGS.INI, so it is still there after you restart. You can look at the file in Notepad, but it is best to change settings with the Control Panel.

Besides the Control Panel's choices, Halcyon remembers where you left windows and how big they were (and whether they were maximized), where you dragged the desktop icons (right-click the desktop and choose Arrange Icons to line them up again), Paint's colours and tools, the folder your last Open or Save dialog was in, and your Sonar level and best times. The file is written safely: a copy is made first and the old one kept as SETTINGS.BAK, so a power cut never leaves you without your settings.
