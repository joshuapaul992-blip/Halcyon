Sonar

Sonar is a puzzle game. Somewhere under the calm water of the bay lie hidden sea mines, and your job is to chart the whole sea floor without setting one off. Start it from the Start menu.

How to play

  • Click a square to send a sonar ping. If there is no mine, the square shows how many of its eight neighbours hide a mine. A square with no mines around it opens up its whole calm neighbourhood for you.
  • Right-click a square where you are sure there is a mine to drop a buoy on it. Right-click again to take the buoy away.
  • When all the mines around a number are marked with buoys, click the number (or click it with the middle button) to ping all its other neighbours at once.
  • You win when every square without a mine has been charted. Hit a mine and the game is over: click the submarine button or press F2 to try again.

Levels and best times

Beginner9 × 9 squares, 10 mines
Intermediate16 × 16 squares, 40 mines
Expert30 × 16 squares, 99 mines

Choose a level with the buttons along the top. The counter on the left shows how many mines are still unmarked and the clock on the right how long you have taken. Sonar remembers your best time for each level, and the level you played last, also after a restart; the ? button shows the times, together with a short how-to.

Tip Your first ping is always safe, so just dive in!
