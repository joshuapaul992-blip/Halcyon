Media Player

Media Player plays sound files. Double-click a .wav file to play it, or start Media Player from the Start menu and choose File > Open. For something to listen to straight away, look in My Documents > Music: Halcyon comes with a short piece of its own.

Playing

Play / PauseStarts and pauses playing (Space).
StopStops and goes back to the start.
The seek barShows where you are in the file; drag it or click it to jump. Left and Right skip 5 seconds back and ahead.
VolumeMedia Player's own volume (Up and Down). It works on top of the computer's master volume, which you set with the speaker icon in the notification area.

The level meter moves with the music, and the window shows the position and the length of the file.

Which files play?

Media Player plays WAV files of almost every kind: CD quality and better, mono or stereo (files with more channels are mixed down), 8 to 32-bit, and the compressed forms that WAV files may carry (IMA ADPCM, μ-law and A-law). MP3 and other compressed music formats are not supported yet.

No sound? Check that the speaker icon is not muted and that the volume is up, then see Sounds and Audio Devices.
