Using Help and Support

You are reading Help and Support right now. Open it from Start > Help and Support, or press F1 in a program to go straight to that program's page.

Finding a topic

The tabs on the left give you three ways to find what you need:

  • Contents lists all the topics by subject, like the chapters of a book. Click a book to open it, then click a topic.
  • Index lists keywords in alphabetical order. Start typing a word and the list narrows down to the matching keywords; double-click one (or press Display). If a keyword appears on more than one page, you choose the page from a short menu.
  • Search looks through the text of every page. Type one or more words and press Enter or List Topics. Pages that contain all the words are listed, the best matches first (a word in the title counts more than a word in the text).

Moving around

Blue underlined words are links: click one to go to another page. The Back and Forward buttons take you through the pages you have seen, and Home returns to the welcome page. Some links start a program for you, such as open Notepad.

Drag the bar between the two panes to make either side wider; Help remembers the width.
