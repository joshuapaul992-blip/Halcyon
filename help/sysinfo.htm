System Information

System Information shows what is inside your computer and how Halcyon is using it: the processor and memory, display adapters, disks and volumes, storage and USB controllers, keyboards and mice, network adapters and sound devices. It is the place to look when you want to know whether Halcyon found a piece of hardware. Open it from the desktop or the Start menu.

Finding your way

  • The tree on the left groups the devices by kind. Click a book to open it.
  • Click a device to see its details on the right: its driver, how it is connected, and what it is doing (a disk's size, a network adapter's link speed, a sound card's outputs...).
  • Computer > Interrupts shows how each device signals the processor and how often: useful when a device does not respond.
  • Kernel Log... shows the system's messages since it started; see The boot log.
  • Refresh reads everything again, for example after plugging something in.

If a device you have is missing from the list, Halcyon has no driver for it yet. Please report it, with your boot log.
